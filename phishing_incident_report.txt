SOC PHISHING EMAIL INVESTIGATION REPORT

Incident Summary

A simulated phishing email was investigated as part of a SOC analyst training exercise. The email used urgency, a threat of account suspension, a suspicious sender address, and an account verification link to encourage the recipient to take action.

Email Details

From: Microsoft Account Security <security-alert@example.com>
To: employee@example.com
Subject: Urgent: Your account will be suspended
Date: 07 October 2026

Initial Assessment:
The email was identified as suspicious due to the sender domain, urgent language, account suspension threat, and verification link.

Investigation Findings

1. URL Analysis – VirusTotal
Finding: The URL returned an HTTP 404 response. No evidence from this result confirmed that the URL was malicious.

2. URL Analysis – URLScan.io
Finding: The scan showed network activity involving 1 IP address and 1 country. No malicious conclusion was made from this scan alone.

3. Email Header Analysis
Finding: The sender claims to represent Microsoft but uses the example.com domain. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC passed, but authentication alone does not prove that the email is legitimate.

4. Base64 Analysis – CyberChef
Finding: The Base64 string decoded to https://example.com.

5. MITRE ATT&CK
Technique: Phishing: Spearphishing Link
Technique ID: T1566.002
Tactic: Initial Access


Severity Assessment

Severity: Medium

Reason: The email contains multiple phishing indicators, including urgency, a threat of account suspension, a suspicious sender, and a verification link. However, the simulated URL analysis did not confirm malicious activity.

Conclusion

The email was assessed as a simulated phishing attempt using social-engineering techniques. The investigation identified several suspicious indicators and mapped the activity to MITRE ATT&CK T1566.002 – Phishing: Spearphishing Link.

Recommended Action

Users should avoid clicking suspicious links and verify account-related requests through the organization's official website or trusted channels.
